# STEAM DESIGN BRIEF

Grounding aesthetic: the receipt is the brand. Treat the game as institutional evidence, not character key art.

## Required deliverables

- Capsule: `460x215`
- Small capsule: `231x87`
- Header: `616x353`
- Main capsule: `467x181`
- Library capsule: `600x900`
- Library hero: `3840x1240`
- Library logo: `1280x720`

## Tone keywords

`institutional` · `deadpan` · `analog-horror-adjacent` · `cactus-not-cute` · `document-not-illustration` · `warm-ink-on-cream`

## Visual direction

- Lead with thermal/receipt language: paper stock, stamps, docket labels, office misregistration, file-cabinet evidence.
- Ed should read as a silhouette first: hat, posture, cigarette. The cigarette is a signature shape, not a glamor object.
- Palette should feel like warm toner on aged office stock: cream, tobacco brown, dried red stamp, dirty green, photocopier black.
- Layouts should feel cropped, filed, or stamped, not painted.

## Reference mood boards

- *Home Safety Hotline* — document-first dread, hotline typography, institutional trust turned uncanny
- *The Mandela Catalogue* — analog evidence framing, bureaucratic unease, dead-serious presentation
- *The Backrooms* (Kane Pixels) — liminal stillness, banal architecture, texture over spectacle

## What to show

- Receipt fragments, stamps, docket marks, seed/case identifiers
- Ed's silhouette in motion or partial profile
- Sparse game geometry as documentary evidence, not HUD-heavy screenshot collage
- Quiet institutional negative space

## What not to do

- No stylized logos
- No render-engine screenshots
- No character portraits
- No 3D
- No cute cactus mascot energy
- No loud horror tropes, blood, glitch overload, or neon

The designer should come back with assets that look like they were pulled from a filing drawer, not a key-art pipeline.
